Use this on any prospect who received one email and went quiet. Suggested trigger: 3 weeks after first send (auto-flagged in outreach_tracker_prefilled.csv as Nov 16 follow-ups).

If it's useful, here's a free calculator that shows the gap between what merchants sign up for and what they actually pay: https://github.com/itzcrazy0/card-processing-rate-calculator

If you'd rather skip it, no hard feelings. If you want to talk, I'm buying coffee: https://calendly.com/dakota-y-startslice/30min

Best,
Dakota Yandle
Slice

## Rules

- Never send more than 3 reactivation emails per prospect.
- Stop immediately if they reply with any interest — move to meeting booked.
- Stop immediately if they unsubscribe or say no.
- Track every send in outreach_tracker.csv with Response Status updated.
- Personalize the local hook in Email 2 with a real nearby business when possible.
